// WHAT THIS SITE COLLECTS

IF YOU CONTACT US, WE SEE YOUR MESSAGE.

Browsing the site itself requires no account and stores no personal data. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us.

CONTACT FORM ▸ EMAIL ▸ LICENSING ▸ NOTHING ELSE
$ what do you store?+

Your name, email address and the contents of your message — only when you email us or use a contact form. That's it.

$ do you use cookies or analytics?+

No advertising cookies and no third-party analytics on this site. Server logs (IP, user-agent) are kept briefly for security and abuse prevention, then rotated.

$ what about my agents' data?+

Nothing. The gateway, its agents, memories and knowledge base run entirely on your hardware. We have no technical ability to access them.

$ do you share data with anyone?+

No. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, except where required by law.
