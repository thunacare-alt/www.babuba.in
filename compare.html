BABUBA VS THE REST.

Three categories, three honest tables. Babuba isn't for everyone — here's exactly where it wins, and where the others are the right call.

  • VS PER-SEAT SAAS
  • VS CODE FRAMEWORKS
  • VS NO-CODE PLATFORMS

VS 01 / PER-SEAT AI SAAS

The chatbot vendors — you rent seats, your data sits in their cloud, and the bill never stops. Babuba is the opposite on every row.

VS 02 / CODE FRAMEWORKS

LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI, AutoGen, Google ADK — excellent libraries. They give you orchestration primitives and make you build everything else yourself.

CAPABILITYBABUBACODE FRAMEWORKS
What you getWorking gateway — agents, router, channels, dashboardCode libraries; you assemble the system
Channels (WhatsApp/Telegram/Gmail)Built-inYou build and maintain them
Admin dashboardIncludedYou build it
Approval workflowsBuilt-in, human-in-the-loopYou implement them
RBAC & guardrailsPer-agent, per-rolePartial / DIY
Audit trailEvery call loggedYou add observability
Team usersUnlimited, scopedNo user model
Deploy effort15 minutesWeeks of engineering
When to pick a framework: you're a software team shipping agents inside your own product and you want full control of every layer. Babuba is for teams that want agents running — not a build project.

VS 03 / NO-CODE PLATFORMS

n8n, Dify, Botpress, Flowise — visual workflow builders. Great for automations; they lean cloud, per-seat pricing and workflow-centric rather than agent-per-department.

CAPABILITYBABUBANO-CODE PLATFORMS
Core modelOne agent per department, code-levelVisual workflows / bots
Self-hostedDefault — your serverMostly cloud; self-host is the paid tier
PricingOne-time, unlimitedPer-seat / per-usage subscriptions
Data controlZero data leavesIn their cloud
GovernanceApprovals, RBAC, guardrails, audit built-inVaries; often add-on
FlexibilityFull API, tools, runtime editsConstrained by the builder
When to pick a no-code platform: you want to chain API calls visually with zero code and don't need per-department agents, deep governance or full data ownership. Babuba's closest relatives here are Dify and Botpress — but those lean cloud and workflow, whereas Babuba is a self-hosted code-level harness with governance built in.

THE BOTTOM LINE

Frameworks are for building. SaaS is for renting. No-code is for wiring. Babuba is for owning — a gateway on your hardware, agents you make yourself, your whole team on it, one-time price. If that's the question you're answering, the table above is the answer.
