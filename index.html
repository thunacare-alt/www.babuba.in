// 06 — CONTROL PLANE

THE AGENTS WORK.
THE HUMANS RULE.

Every agent runs inside a control plane you define — who can do what, what an agent may never do, and what must come back to a human before it ships.

RBAC.MATRIX

WHO CAN DO WHAT

Role-based access per agent — super_admin, admin, manager, user, viewer. Scope access to departments, agents and actions. One agent, whole team, exact permissions each.

GUARDRAIL.RULES

WHAT AGENTS MAY NEVER DO

Hard allow / deny / warn rules per agent. Deny payments above a limit. Deny deleting records. Deny sending without approval. The rule is enforced in code, not in the prompt.

APPROVAL.CHAIN

NOTHING SHIPS SILENTLY

Agent prepares and verifies. The action lands on your phone with the full picture — one tap to approve, one tap to reject. Approval gates on payments, exports, sends.

AUDIT.LOG

EVERY ACTION, RECORDED

Every agent action is logged and searchable — who, what, when, which rule allowed it. Export the log any time. Roll back, fix the rule, move on.

// 08 — MAN BABUBA

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

$ man babuba --data+

Does my data leave my server?

No. The gateway, agents and database run on your machine. Your data never goes to our cloud — you can even run it fully air-gapped.

$ man babuba --model+

Which LLM does it use?

Bring your own — OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. No lock-in; swap models per agent.

$ man babuba --install+

How long does deployment take?

About fifteen minutes on a standard server or desktop. One install command, then connect your channels and boot agents.

$ man babuba --agents+

Can agents really approve things?

They prepare, verify and flag. Humans approve — every action requiring approval lands on your phone with the full picture. One tap to approve, one tap to reject.

$ man babuba --limit+

What if an agent does something wrong?

Guardrails, role-based access and approval gates contain it. Every action is logged and searchable — roll back, fix the rule, move on.

$ man babuba --why+

Why an OS and not an app?

Because agents aren't a feature — they become the way your company works. That needs a system layer: one router, many processes, shared memory, full audit. That's what an OS is.

$ man babuba --license+

What does the ₹5,50,000 license include?

The full OS — unlimited agents (make as many as you need), workflows, approvals, guardrails, RBAC, every channel, on your own server. Build anything on top of it: no restrictions, no conditions, no per-seat fees, no renewal. One payment, yours for life.

$ man babuba --users+

How many users can use one agent?

As many as you need — unlimited. One agent serves the whole team, each user with their own role and permissions. No seating costs, no per-user fees, ever. The license covers the OS, not heads.
