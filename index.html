One agent. Every department. Total control.

04 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my agent really isolated?

Yes. Every client gets a private tenant gateway — your own instance, database, memory and channels. Nothing is shared between tenants.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The guided chat configures everything — purpose, policies, guardrails, roles. If you can answer questions, you can deploy an agent.

What channels can I connect?

WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, Slack and your website. Connect one or all — the same agent answers everywhere.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month, cancel in one click. Your data is exported and deleted on request.

What happens if the agent hits something sensitive?

Your guardrails define the line. Anything flagged routes to a human approver on WhatsApp/Telegram — nothing slips through.
