02 — WHAT IS BABUBA

One brain. A team of agents. Your business, automated.

Babuba is an AI workforce you can deploy in an afternoon. Not a single chatbot — a team of specialist agents, one per department: support, sales, finance, HR, IT. A smart router sends every message to the right expert automatically.

Your agents live where your customers and team already are — WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, Slack and your website — with one shared conversation memory across all of them.

Babuba doesn't just talk. It acts: it creates documents, analyses spreadsheets, runs scheduled reports, triggers workflows — with human approval gates where it matters. And it learns your business: long-term memory, knowledge bases, RAG over your own documents.

Because it's self-hosted, it runs on your VM with your model keys. No SaaS lock-in. No data leaving your infrastructure. Ever.
