// 07 — Asked first

Plain words.

What Babuba is, where your data lives, and how it fits into a team that already has a hundred things running.

Is Ghost the same thing as Babuba?+

No. Babuba is the framework — the gateway, router, agents, RBAC, guardrails and workflows you install on your own server. Ghost is a product we built on top of Babuba: one packaged AI employee for WhatsApp, sold monthly, run by us.

Can I get Ghost on my own server?+

Yes. Ghost runs on Babuba, so a Babuba licence lets you host the same stack yourself and build your own agents beside it.

Does Ghost act without asking?+

He is guardrailed. Anything that sends money, books a job or commits a price above your threshold stops and asks for approval on WhatsApp — one tap to approve or reject, and every action is logged with an IST timestamp.

Who is behind both?+

Thuna Care Private Limited, Gurugram. Babuba is deployed with companies running their own agent teams; Ghost is the first product shipped on the framework.
