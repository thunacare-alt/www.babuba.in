// 07 — FAQ

BABUBA, IN PLAIN WORDS.

What Babuba is, where your data lives, and how it fits into your team.

What is Babuba?

Babuba is a self-hosted AI agent platform (agent gateway). You run it on your own server, make one AI agent per department — finance, ops, MIS, admin — and give your whole team access with no per-seat fees.

How is Babuba different from a SaaS chatbot?

SaaS rents you a dashboard in someone else's cloud and bills per seat. Babuba runs on your own hardware, with agents you make yourself and as many users as you need. Zero data leaves your server.

Where does my data stay?

On your server. Not on Babuba's cloud, not on the model vendor's. Babuba is self-hosted, so zero data leaves your machine.

Which channels can I talk to my agents on?

WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, and web chat. The Babuba gateway connects to the channels you already use.

How many agents and users can I have?

Unlimited. Create one agent per department — as many as you need — and add unlimited users per agent with RBAC-scoped roles. No per-agent or per-seat fees, ever.

How fast is deployment?

Seconds. babuba agents create --name finance --dept accounts brings an agent online in about 2 seconds. No infra, no deployments.

What governance is built in?

Each agent is an isolated process with its own memory, tools, guardrails and approval rules — auditable and replaceable. RBAC scoping and an approval queue are built in, and agents ask before anything ships.
