// 04 — THE VISION

AN AI WORKFORCE YOU OWN.

Today, adopting AI means renting intelligence from a vendor who keeps your data, prices per seat, and can change the rules whenever it likes. We think the opposite model wins: the intelligence is rented, the workforce is owned. Babuba exists to make that practical for a company of twenty people, not just a company of twenty thousand — one process, your hardware, your models, your audit trail, and an employee you can actually afford to hire.

The end state is a company where every department already has an agent that has read the files, remembers the history, and answers inside the apps people already use. No new interface to learn. No data leaving the building.
