// 05 — FAQ

GHOST AND BABUBA, IN PLAIN WORDS.

Is Ghost the same thing as Babuba?

No. Babuba is the framework — the gateway, router, agents, RBAC, guardrails and workflows you install on your own server. Ghost is a product we built on top of Babuba: one packaged AI employee for WhatsApp, sold monthly, run by us.

Can I get Ghost on my own server?

Yes. Ghost runs on Babuba, so a Babuba license lets you host the same stack yourself and build your own agents beside it.

Does Ghost act without asking?

He is guardrailed. Anything that sends money, books a job or commits a price above your threshold stops and asks for approval on WhatsApp, one tap to approve or reject — and every action is logged.

Who is behind both?

Thuna Care Private Limited. Babuba is deployed with companies running their own agent teams; Ghost is the first product shipped on the framework.
