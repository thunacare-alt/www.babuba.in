// THE FLOORS

WHAT EACH ONE
ACTUALLY OWNS.

Not "capabilities". The actual work, the actual files it touches, and the line it will not cross without your tap.

SALES.ENQUIRIES ONLINE
  • Answers every enquiry in under a minute — 11pm the same as 11am
  • Qualifies, quotes from your price list, books the slot
  • Chases the one that went quiet, hands you the one that needs a human

reads: price list · calendar · past orders

FIN.ACCOUNTS ONLINE
  • Invoice capture and validation, GST reconciliation against filings
  • Who owes what, how long, and the polite reminder on schedule
  • Daily cash close; every payment above threshold waits for your approval

writes: ledger · reminder queue · approval requests

OPS.LOGISTICS ONLINE
  • Dispatch tracking, freight bills checked line by line
  • Pending pickups chased on WhatsApp, reorder alerts before the shelf is empty
  • Escalates the job that sat too long instead of logging it twice

reads: dispatch board · vendor POs · stock levels

HR.PEOPLE ONLINE
  • Attendance on WhatsApp, leave requests, approvals and balances
  • Shift and duty reminders, monthly salary statement
  • New-joiner document checklist; flags a pattern before it becomes a resignation

writes: register · leave ledger · payroll pack

MARKETING ONLINE
  • Win-backs, festival and quiet-hour offers targeted by buying history
  • Review requests, birthdays and anniversaries, never a blast to everyone
  • Reports which offer actually brought money back

reads: customer history · campaign results

ADMIN.COMPLIANCE ONLINE
  • Contract and renewal reminders, regulatory deadlines tracked
  • Filing dates handed to the accountant on time, every time
  • Audit trail searchable by session, tool and cost

writes: deadline register · filing pack · audit index

SUPPORT.CARE ONLINE
  • Triages what comes in, answers the known ones from your own material
  • Escalates with the full thread and what it already tried
  • Never invents a policy, a price or a promise

reads: knowledge base · open tickets

BUY.PROCUREMENT ONLINE
  • Three quotes, compared on the terms that matter, not the headline price
  • Raises the PO, chases delivery, matches the bill to the PO
  • Nothing gets ordered without the approval you set

writes: quote sheet · PO · GRN match

YOUR.DEPARTMENT ONLINE
  • Name it, describe the job in a sentence, give it a toolbelt
  • Set the rules it may not cross — it obeys them at the gate, not in the prompt
  • Inherits the router, the approvals and the audit trail on day one

// BETWEEN FLOORS

ONE AGENT FINISHES.
ANOTHER PICKS IT UP.

The work does not stop at a department boundary and neither does the thread. A handoff is a task with the context attached — not a message forwarded to a human.

01

Enquiry lands

sales.enquiries answers, quotes, and takes the booking.

02

Money is involved

It hands fin.accounts the order, the amount and the customer's history.

03

Threshold crossed

fin.accounts stops and asks you — one tap on WhatsApp, from your phone.

04

It ships, or it doesn't

Approved: invoice out, dispatch told. Refused: the reason is written down.

// UNDER EVERY DESK

THE SAME FOUR THINGS,
ON EVERY FLOOR.

RULES
GATE NOT PROMPT
What it may say, what it may touch, what it must never promise. Enforced before the action ships — not trusted to the model to remember.
AUDIT
100% LOGGED
Every session, tool call, rupee and approval with an IST timestamp. Searchable months later, by session or by cost.
PERMISSIONS
1 FLOOR EACH
Roles decide who may read a floor and who may approve on it. One agent never opens another department's data.
LOCATION
YOUR SERVER
Every floor runs inside your hardware. Nothing leaves the building to be answered, and nothing needs our cloud to stay up.
