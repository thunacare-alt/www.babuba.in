01 / WHAT WE BUILD

We sell two things, and only one of them is a platform. Babuba Gateway is the engine: a single process that hosts many AI agents — one per department or function — connects them to WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, Slack and web chat, and gives them roles, memory, tools, guardrails and an audit trail. It runs on hardware you control.

GHOST is that same engine sold as a subscription product. Sold at a flat monthly price, GHOST is a managed AI employee that answers customers, runs daily operations, writes to the books and posts reports inside WhatsApp. Same engine underneath, sold as an outcome instead of software — one codebase, two price points.

  • Babuba Gateway — self-hosted platform. One-time licence, no per-agent or per-seat fees, your infrastructure, your models.
  • GHOST — AI employee on WhatsApp. One per-agent monthly subscription, standard jobs ship on day one, no infrastructure to own.
  • One codebase — GHOST runs on the Babuba gateway, router, agent runtime, scheduler, approval engine and dashboard.
Why both exist: some customers want to own the software and run it behind their own firewall. Others want the work done, with nothing to install. Babuba covers the first, GHOST covers the second — and every GHOST deployment is also a live reference for the gateway.

02 / HOW GHOST SITS ON BABUBA

There is no second platform. Each GHOST customer is one isolated instance of the same gateway, with its own container, credentials and data.

  • The employee — a Babuba agent with its own identity, memory and tool allowlist.
  • WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail — Babuba channel adapters. The chat app is the interface; nothing to install.
  • The morning and evening reports — Babuba cron jobs on fixed schedules, delivered to the owner's group.
  • Approvals before money moves — Babuba guardrails and the approval engine, on by default.
  • Google Sheets, Drive and mail — Babuba tool integrations, authorised by the customer and revocable at any time.
  • "See his logs, change his rules" — the Babuba admin panel, one per customer.
  • Isolation — one gateway and one data store per customer; no agent can read another customer's data.

04 / INFRASTRUCTURE WE RUN

GHOST is live today, not a prototype: customer agents answering real WhatsApp messages, running scheduled operational jobs and writing to real spreadsheets. Running that means compute per customer, managed databases, model inference, document storage and mail integration — the part of the stack we operate so the customer does not have to.

Babuba customers run the same software on their own machines, which is why the platform is packaged as a single deployable process rather than a cloud account. Both models share one engineering team and one codebase.

05 / HOW WE WORK

We sell software the old way: you buy a licence or a subscription, you get a working system, you talk to the engineers who build it. There is no per-seat rent and no switch we can flip to turn a deployment off.

What we ask in return is that the licence is respected: no resale, no hosting it for third parties, no stripping the activation check. Those limits are in the terms, in plain language.
