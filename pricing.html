// MAN BABUBA

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

$ man babuba --data+

Does my data leave my server?

No. The gateway, agents and database run on your machine. Your data never goes to our cloud — you can even run it fully air-gapped.

$ man babuba --model+

Which LLM does it use?

Bring your own — OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. No lock-in; swap models per agent.

$ man babuba --install+

How long does deployment take?

About fifteen minutes on a standard server or desktop. One install command, then connect your channels and boot agents.

$ man babuba --agents+

Can agents really approve things?

They prepare, verify and flag. Humans approve — every action requiring approval lands on your phone with the full context. One tap to approve, one tap to reject.

$ man babuba --users+

How many users can an agent serve?

Unlimited. Add your whole team by phone or Telegram ID, scope each one with RBAC, and they all work the same agent — no per-seat fees, ever.

$ man babuba --license+

What does the license cover?

Everything. Unlimited agents, unlimited users, all channels, workflows, approvals, guardrails, RBAC and updates for life — no restrictions, no conditions.

$ man babuba --pay+

How do I pay?

UPI, bank transfer or card — one-time, nothing recurring. You get a GST invoice on request and your license key instantly.

$ man babuba --refund+

What if it's not for me?

New licenses carry a 14-day money-back guarantee. Email support@babuba.ai and we'll refund in full — no questions asked. Full terms on the refund page.

$ man babuba --updates+

What does "updates for life" mean?

Every future version of Babuba — new features, new channels, new model support — is included in your license, free, forever. One-time price, no subscription, ever.

$ man babuba --hardware+

What hardware do I need?

A standard server or desktop — 4+ GB RAM, 10 GB disk, any OS that runs Node.js (Linux recommended). Bring your own LLM keys, or run fully offline with Ollama.

$ man babuba --support+

What support do I get?

Priority support for life — real engineers at support@babuba.ai, not a ticket black hole. Setup and migration help included with the license.

$ man babuba --refund+

Is there a guarantee?

Yes — 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If Babuba doesn't fit your workflow, you get a full refund.
