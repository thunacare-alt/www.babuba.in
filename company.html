01 / WHAT WE BUILD

Babuba Gateway is a single process that hosts many AI agents — one per department or function — and routes incoming work to the right one. It connects to WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, Slack and web chat, and runs entirely on hardware you control: your server, your cloud account, or a desktop machine.

  • Agents per department — each with its own identity, memory, tools and guardrails. Unlimited agents, unlimited users.
  • Your infrastructure — your database, your logs, your backups. Nothing is routed through our systems.
  • Your models — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or local models via Ollama.
  • Governance by default — role-based access, per-agent guardrails, approval gates on high-risk actions, and a searchable audit trail.
  • One-time license — priced once per organisation, not per seat. No recurring rent.
Everything above ships in one install. There is no cloud account to create with us and no usage metering on our side — the software runs on your hardware, under your control.

02 / WHO WE SERVE

Our customers run operations where an AI agent touching an invoice, a dispatch record or a customer message has consequences. Manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, distribution, retail and professional services — organisations that need the automation but cannot send their data to someone else's cloud.

They come to Babuba because the alternatives are a per-seat subscription that gets expensive as the team grows, or a framework that needs a software team to build on it. Babuba is neither: it is a working system you install and own.

04 / HOW WE WORK

We sell software the old way: you buy a license, you get the software, you run it. Support comes from the engineers who build it, not a ticket queue. There is no forced upgrade path and no switch we can flip to turn your deployment off — a license you hold keeps working on your server.

What we ask in return is that the license is respected: no resale, no hosting it for third parties, no stripping the activation check. Those limits are in the terms, in plain language.
